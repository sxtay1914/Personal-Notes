## 68. Text Justification
* Pretty tedious problem, not too hard and not too easy, medium-hard. The catch here is to ensure the space is taken into consideration when calculating current total for example for three words need to add additional 2 because fo the spaces

* TC: O(N * maxWidth) not O(N**2) because string in py is immutable. eg. 1 + 2 + .. + maxWidth
* SC: O(total char)
