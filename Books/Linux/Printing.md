Chapter 22 Printing 
## Commands
1. pr: convert text files for printing 
2. lpr: print files
3. a2ps: format files for printing on a Postscript printer (anything to Postscript)
4. lpstat: printing status
5. lpq: printing queue
6. lprm or cancel: printing remove jobs

## CUPS & RIP
1. What is CUPS for? to manage printing jobs
2. RIP? PostScript interpreter
